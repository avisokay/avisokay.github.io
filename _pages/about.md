I am a Bellwether Postdoctoral Scholar at the University of California, Berkeley, supervised by
[Joshua Blumenstock](https://www.jblumenstock.com/). I recently completed my Ph.D. in Sociology at the
University of Washington, co-advised by [Sasha Johfre](https://www.sashajohfre.com/) and
[Tyler McCormick](https://www.stat.washington.edu/~tylermc/), and am an affiliated student with the
International Max Planck Research School for Population, Health and Data Science (IMPRS-PHDS).

My research explores how to leverage computational methods and statistics to study social phenomena,
typically motivated by questions in public health, economics, and sociology. I am particularly
interested in how to perform valid inference using predictions from black box algorithms, and how to
use AI/ML methods in a way that is both transparent and socially grounded. My work has appeared at
venues including EMNLP, COLM, and the ACL Workshop on NLP+CSS.
